# College discovery handoff test

This eight-turn case exercises a bounded causal-discovery sidecar followed by
independent causal review and one approved observational analysis. Exact prompts
and artifact expectations live in [`test-cases.json`](test-cases.json). Apply
the shared rules in [`evaluation-guide.md`](evaluation-guide.md).

| Checkpoint | Turns | Required result |
|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | 1-2 | Domain constraints and audited data readiness are established without discovery, analysis, or output. |
| 2 | 3-4 | Discovery is scoped before execution. One artifact follows the exact controller-bound contract and keeps every edge or orientation candidate-only. |
| 3 | 5 | Causal review independently assesses timing, adjustment validity, design fit, and claim strength. Discovery neither selects an adjustment set nor opens the analysis gate. |
| 4 | 6-7 | One ready analysis scope preserves that review boundary, and exact approval creates one matching analysis artifact. |
| 5 | 8 | Decision synthesis separates discovery hypotheses, completed analysis evidence, and unresolved assumptions without new output. |

Judge discovery fidelity from the actual method, variable treatment,
constraints, stability checks, and output, not from a copied contract. Do not
grade the exact learned graph or estimate when the bounded workflow and claim
boundary are preserved.
