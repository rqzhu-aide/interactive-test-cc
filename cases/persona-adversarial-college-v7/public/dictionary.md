# College data notes

This supplied CSV has one row per institution and a label column named rownames.
The ISLR package describes 777 institutions and 18 variables from the 1995
U.S. News college publication.

- Expend: instructional spending per student.
- Grad.Rate: the reported graduation percentage.
- Private: Yes identifies private institutions; No identifies public institutions.
- Top10perc and Top25perc: percentages of entering students in the respective high-school class-rank groups.
- S.F.Ratio: students per faculty member.
- F.Undergrad and P.Undergrad: full-time and part-time undergraduate counts.
- Outstate: tuition charged to out-of-state students.
- Enroll: entering-student count.

Source documentation: https://stat.ethz.ch/CRAN/web/packages/ISLR/refman/ISLR.html
